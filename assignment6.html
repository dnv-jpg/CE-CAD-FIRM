Assignment 6 – Social Networking Assignment

IE492 Spring 2026 – Dr. Ranky

Introduction

This assignment focuses on analyzing current engineering-related topics through social networking discussions and articles. In engineering management, it is important not only to understand technical concepts but also to stay aware of how new technologies and real-world issues are impacting the industry. These discussions help connect classroom knowledge to practical applications, especially in areas such as automation, energy systems, and cybersecurity. By reviewing and discussing these articles, it becomes easier to understand how engineering decisions are made based on efficiency, cost, performance, and risk. These topics are also important because they show how engineering is constantly evolving and how companies must adapt to new technologies to remain competitive. The ideas in this assignment are also applied to D&V Civil Engineering Management, which focuses on CAD modeling, BIM coordination, and sustainable design.

Discussion Partner

Abigail Deitz

Article 1 — Executive Summary

Former Citi Exec: Robots Will Soon Outnumber Human Employees

This article explains how artificial intelligence and robotics are expected to become more common in the workforce over time. It discusses the idea that robots may eventually outnumber human workers because they are becoming more efficient and less expensive to use. One important point in the article is that robots can perform repetitive tasks and may be able to replace certain types of jobs, especially in industries that rely on routine work. During the discussion with Abigail Dietz, we talked about how this change will not happen immediately but will develop gradually as technology improves. We also discussed how current robots, such as humanoid robots and automated systems, are already being used in industries like manufacturing and logistics, but they still have limitations when it comes to complex tasks. From an engineering perspective, this article relates to how companies use technology to improve efficiency. The main idea is that if a robot can perform a task faster and at a lower cost than a human worker, then companies may choose to use automation. This is based on comparing costs and performance, which is an important part of engineering decision-making. In D&V Civil Engineering Management, this concept can be applied to CAD modeling and BIM coordination. While robots may not replace engineers directly, automation tools and software can improve efficiency in design work. For example, automated clash detection in BIM can reduce errors and save time. This means engineers can focus more on design and coordination instead of repetitive tasks. My takeaway from this article is that technology will continue to improve and become more integrated into engineering work. Instead of replacing engineers completely, it will change how engineers work and help them complete tasks more efficiently. Companies like D&V should continue to use new tools and technologies to stay competitive while still relying on human expertise for decision-making and design.

Article 2 — Executive Summary

High-Temperature Superconductors Could Redefine Data Center Power Density

This article explains how high-temperature superconductors (HTS) could improve the way electricity is delivered, especially in data centers that require large amounts of power. The main idea is that current electrical systems lose energy due to resistance, which reduces efficiency. As technology continues to grow, especially with artificial intelligence systems, the demand for energy is increasing, and existing systems may not be able to keep up. One important point in the article is that traditional materials like copper and aluminum create resistance when electricity flows through them, which causes energy loss. In contrast, superconductors allow electricity to move without resistance when they are cooled to very low temperatures. This makes them more efficient and allows them to carry more power in a smaller space. During the discussion with Abigail Dietz, we talked about how this technology could improve infrastructure systems but also comes with challenges. One of the main challenges is the cost of implementing superconductors and the need for cryogenic cooling systems. Because of this, the technology is not widely used yet, but it has strong potential for future applications. From an engineering perspective, this article relates to improving system efficiency and reducing energy loss. Engineers must analyze whether the benefits of new technology are worth the cost. This involves comparing performance improvements with implementation expenses. In D&V Civil Engineering Management, this concept can be applied to improving efficiency in design and coordination processes. While superconductors are not directly used in CAD or BIM work, the idea of reducing inefficiencies and improving performance is very relevant. For example, optimizing workflows and reducing design errors can improve overall project efficiency in a similar way that superconductors improve energy efficiency. My takeaway from this article is that engineering solutions are always evolving, and new technologies can improve performance significantly. However, engineers must carefully evaluate cost and practicality before implementing new systems. Companies like D&V can apply this mindset by continuously improving workflows and adopting new technologies when they provide clear benefits.

Article 3 — Executive Summary

Microsoft: Critical Security Issue Found in Windows Notepad

This article explains a security issue found in the updated version of Windows Notepad. The vulnerability allows an attacker to execute code if a user opens a malicious Markdown file and clicks a link inside the file. While this may seem surprising because Notepad is a simple program, the issue comes from new features that were added, such as Markdown support and clickable links. During the discussion with Abigail Dietz, we talked about how adding new features to software can increase its complexity and create new risks. In this case, Notepad became more advanced, but this also introduced a security vulnerability. We also discussed how the issue requires user interaction, meaning that the user must open the file and click the link for the attack to work. From an engineering perspective, this article shows how system complexity can affect reliability and safety. As systems become more advanced, they require more testing and validation to ensure they are secure. Engineers must consider not only performance but also potential risks when designing systems. In D&V Civil Engineering Management, this concept applies to digital tools such as CAD software and BIM platforms. These tools are used daily for design and coordination, and if they are not secure, it could affect project data and workflow. This means that engineers must be careful when using files from external sources and ensure that systems are updated regularly. My takeaway from this article is that even simple tools can become complex and require careful management. Engineers must balance functionality and security, especially when using digital tools. Companies like D&V should focus on maintaining secure systems and educating team members about potential risks when working with project files.

Conclusion

This social networking assignment showed how current engineering topics connect to engineering management decisions. The articles discussed automation, energy efficiency, and cybersecurity, which are all important areas in modern engineering. Each topic shows that engineers must think about more than just technical performance. They also have to consider cost, risk, efficiency, and long-term impacts when making decisions. In D&V Civil Engineering Management, these ideas connect to CAD modeling, BIM coordination, and sustainable design. Automation can improve design efficiency, energy-related technology shows the importance of reducing waste, and cybersecurity reminds engineers to protect project data. Overall, this assignment helped show that engineering management requires staying aware of new technology and understanding how it can improve project performance while still managing risks.
